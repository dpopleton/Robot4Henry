## Decided
- **The head is a beam-and-corner frame:** 25 mm star-profile beams, outside
  corners held with **M3 bolts into heat-set inserts**; M2 inserts and bolts mount the electronics. Panels on the left and
  right; the top and bottom get their own designs. Front is one plate with
  mounting holes for every component (R-212).
- **The head has its own USB hub and sound card;** one USB and one power lead
  leave through the bottom (D-013, R-213). The power supply is still open.
- **Two speakers** in compartments in the front plate's bottom corners, the
  mouth screen between them (R-306).
- **Wake button hidden in the crown;** the crown sits on a top panel. Which
  Pico reads the button is open (R-308).
- **Touch sensor on top of the head**, new idea (R-313).
- Each Pico sits on its own organ.
- No toddler-proofing requirements for the head: Henry's sister keeps away
  from the build area.
- Camera type still undecided (R-601).
