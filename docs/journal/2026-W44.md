- **Robustness fixes from agent code reviews.** Dan was testing agents as a
  work side project and kept the fixes that held up (uncommitted, 215 tests pass):
  - a lock around the shared SQLite connections (half of R-110, now in progress);
  - rest no longer drops a session's memories when the reply just contains
    the word "none", a failed summary keeps the old one, storage paths are
    absolute and can be overridden, a bad port setting falls back to the
    fake port, and malformed serial lines are dropped (R-111).

## Built — physical
- **Salvaged a broken USB/Bluetooth soundbar.** Only its case was damaged
  (discarded). Kept and tested working:
  - 2 × speakers, 4 Ω 5 W, 5.5 cm across (the face openings were designed at 5 cm)
  - the **Jieli R30 PLUS** amp/controller board (USB + Bluetooth) with a rotary-encoder volume knob
  - 2 × U-shaped WS2812-type LED boards, 9 LEDs each, ~35 × 60 mm
- **Tests on Ubuntu:** the board is a standard USB sound card with no
  drivers (`card 2: PLUS [R30 PLUS]`, USB ID `5943:3130`).
  `speaker-test -D plughw:CARD=PLUS,DEV=0 -c 2 -t wav` played cleanly on both
  channels. `amixer` shows only mute switches, so the host can't set the
  volume. The chip forgets the volume at power-off, and its default is too quiet.

## Decided
- **Personality sheet approved** (R-106): Qbot is a boy, Henry's age, who
  crash-landed from the sun.
- **Wire colours mean a job** (power, ground, clock, data, control, select),
  the same on every organ (D-019). The eyes are wired without a backlight
  wire for now, so dimming waits for it (R-206).
- **The soundbar's board and speakers replace the Picade Max and 40 mm
  drivers** (D-017). The **mouth Pico emulates the volume knob** after every
  power-up; its click count is the hardware volume ceiling. A USB dongle +
  PAM8403 is the fallback.
- **Speakers:** each in its own box behind the 1.5 cm face, with a bevel to
  take the bigger frame. Nothing sticks out; the aluminium stays flush with
  parallel slots over each speaker (R-306).
- **Bluetooth** stays for now; deciding what to do about it before
  unsupervised use is an idea item (R-314).
- **Lights:** one U board behind the Q, the other lighting the inside of the
  head through the clear back, chained on the lights Pico's one 3-wire
  connector (connectors already in hand). Powered from VBUS with a hard
  brightness cap (D-018). Glow mock-up first, then Henry approves (R-211).
- Power: the sound board stays on one ~1 A hub port; measure it before
  adding any 5V feed (R-213).
