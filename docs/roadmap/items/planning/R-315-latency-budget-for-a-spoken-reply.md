## Goal
Measure how long each step of a spoken reply takes on the robot laptop
(speech-to-text → LLM → text-to-speech) and find out what Henry will wait for.

## Why
A reply under ~1s feels alive; 5s+ feels broken to a 7-year-old. This
decides when the brain computer needs upgrading (R-902). Split from R-105 in
the Phase 1 grill, since it needs Phase 3's ears and voice.
