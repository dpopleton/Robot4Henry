## Goal
A 25×25×15 cm head built as a frame: 25 mm star-profile beams (8 × 20 cm,
4 × 10 cm) joined by 8 outside corners with M3 bolts into heat-set inserts,
closed by a truss panel on each side, a top panel the crown sits on, a bottom
panel, a front plate with mounting holes for each component, and a back with
an access door and an acrylic window.

## Why
Everything in Phase 2-3 is mounted in it as it's developed. Bolted panels mean
one face can be reprinted without touching the rest.
