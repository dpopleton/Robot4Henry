## Goal
In quiet times, rest turns many small memories into a few bigger ones
("Henry talks a lot about his cat, Lego and dinosaurs").

## Why
Even without duplicates, memories pile up as single sentences. Merging
related ones (often called reflection or consolidation) keeps what matters
findable and the store small. Not needed until there's enough history.

## Tasks
- [ ] Wait until there are a few weeks of real memories to look at
- [ ] Group related memories, summarise each group, keep links to the originals
- [ ] Runs only while Qbot is asleep or idle, never during a conversation
- [ ] Check merged memories against the same safety check as other memories (R-109)

## Done when
After a few weeks of chats, the prompt can pick up a merged memory that
covers several conversations.
