## Goal
A speaker at the bottom centre of the head, firing down and forward, in a foam-packed enclosure, with an amplifier.

## Why
Qbot needs a voice; the foam stops sound carrying into the head and the mics.

## Tasks
- [ ] Pick speaker + amp
- [ ] Decide how audio gets from the laptop (headphone jack → amp, or a USB sound card)
- [ ] Enclosure + foam
- [ ] Mount in the shell

## Done when
Qbot's audio plays clearly from the head without buzzing the shell.

## Open questions
- How does sound get from the laptop to the speaker?
