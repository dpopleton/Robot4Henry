## Goal
Make the head one tidy, finished unit in its thin aluminium skin, with the crown on top: cable management (it shows through the clear back), strain relief, final prints.

## Why
Everything was mounted as it was developed; this is the cleanup.

## Tasks
- [ ] Tidy wiring (visible through the clear back panel)
- [ ] One USB lead and one power lead to the body/laptop (R-213)
- [ ] Thin aluminium skin (bought): cut, fit, holes for the mics and front panels; the crown sits on top of it
- [ ] Every head panel designed and printed (bottom panel comes with the neck, R-401)
- [ ] Back door hinge pin fitted

## Done when
Every head panel is designed and printed (bar the bottom), the aluminium skin is on, the wiring behind the clear panel is tidy, the hinge pin is in, and only one USB lead and one power lead leave the head.
