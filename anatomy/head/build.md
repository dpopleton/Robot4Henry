# Head — build notes

The physical side of the head: what it's made of, how it's wired and assembled, and what went wrong. For Henry-facing slideshow text see `manifest.yaml`; for the code see the `organ` in `part.yaml`.

## What it is
A 3D-printed box, 25 × 25 × 15 cm (W × H × D), holding the eyes, mouth, Q and
antenna lights, crown mics and the speaker. The brain computer (an old
laptop) sits beside it on cables until the body exists.

## Status
- [x] Sides printed
- [x] Back modelled: access door with a **clear panel** (Henry's idea — so he
      can see inside) and plenty of vents
- [ ] Back printed, assembled and tested
- [ ] Front frame modelled: bolt-in mounts for each component — eyes, mouth,
      Q, and **space for a camera at the top of the front face, above the Q**
      (R-601)
- [ ] Speaker mount: bottom centre, firing down and forward, in a
      foam-packed enclosure (R-306)
- [ ] Top of the head + antenna sketched
- [ ] Crown — designed after the mic boards are in hand (see `../crown/`)

## Design notes
- The front is a **frame with bolt-in mounts**, so a component can be swapped
  without reprinting the shell.
- The inside is visible through the back door — keep wiring tidy (R-402).
- Re-flashing firmware happens a lot: keep the Picos' USB ports and BOOTSEL
  buttons reachable through the back door.
